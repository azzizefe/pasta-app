LUMIÈRE SUCRÉE

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Welcome to Lumière Sucrée ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our iOS application collects, uses, and safeguards your personal data in full compliance with the Apple App Store Guidelines and applicable privacy laws.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Identity Information: When you sign in using Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In, we receive your name and obfuscated/verified email address provided by the identity provider. We never have access to your Apple or Google passwords.
  • User-Provided Photos & Prompts: If you choose to upload an inspiration photo (e.g., wedding invitation, dress, or color theme) or provide custom text prompts, these assets are transmitted securely via TLS encryption solely to generate your custom cake design. Photos are processed ephemerally and are not used to train public AI models.
  • Purchases & In-App Transactions: All financial transactions are processed directly by Apple through StoreKit 2 / Apple In-App Purchases. We do not collect, store, or have access to your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or banking details.
  • Diagnostic & Performance Data: We may collect anonymized crash reports and app performance metrics via Sentry to maintain stability and prevent application errors.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected strictly for:

  • Rendering your customized haute patisserie 3D/8K cake designs.
  • Managing your credit balance and saved creations on your local device and secure account.
  • Providing customer support and processing legitimate credit refund requests for failed generations.
  • Ensuring compliance with Apple App Store policies and security protocols.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is shared only with essential infrastructure service providers necessary to operate the application:

  • Apple Inc.: For secure authentication (Sign in with Apple) and in-app purchase processing.
  • AI Infrastructure Providers (Google Cloud & Gemini): For transient generative processing of cake artwork under strict data protection terms.
  • Sentry: For anonymized crash diagnostics and error tracking.

4. Account Deletion & Data Retention

You have full control over your data. In accordance with Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v):

  • You can permanently delete your account and all stored preferences instantly at any time from within the app: Profile Tab > Delete Account.
  • Upon deletion, your local data, cached creations, and authentication tokens are immediately removed from your device.

5. Children's Privacy

Lumière Sucrée is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.
